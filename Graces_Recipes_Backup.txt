Bacon & Scallop Mushroom Risotto
  with Parsley & Lemon Wedges
  Section: Pasta & Risotto
  (prep 10 min, cook 45 min, 1030 cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Chicken Stock Concentrates: 2 / 4
    - Shallot: 1 / 2
    - Lemon: 1 / 2
    - Scallops: 10 oz / 20 oz
    - Bacon: 4 oz / 8 oz
    - Arborio Rice: 3/4 cup / 1.5 cups
    - Button Mushrooms: 4 oz / 8 oz
    - Parsley: 1/4 oz / 1/2 oz
    - Italian Cheese Blend: 1/4 cup / 1 cup
    - Garlic: 1 clove / 2 cloves
    - Butter, oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  Instructions:
    SIMMER STOCK & COOK BACON: In a medium pot, combine stock concentrates and 5 cups water (8 cups for 4 servings). Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer. Heat a large, dry pan over medium-high heat. Add bacon; cook, turning occasionally and adjusting heat if browning too quickly, until crispy, 6-10 minutes. Turn off heat; transfer bacon to a paper-towel-lined plate. Once cool enough to handle, roughly chop. Wipe out pan.
    START PREP: While bacon cooks, wash and dry produce. Peel and very thinly slice garlic. Halve, peel, and mince shallot.
    COOK RISOTTO: Heat a drizzle of oil in a second large pan over medium heat. Add garlic and shallot; cook, stirring, until softened, 1 minute. Add rice; stir until translucent, 1-2 minutes. Add 1 cup stock (1.5 cups for 4 servings); stir until liquid has mostly absorbed. Repeat with remaining stock — adding 1/2 cup at a time and stirring until liquid has mostly absorbed — until rice is al dente and mixture is creamy, 18-20 minutes.
    FINISH PREP & COOK MUSHROOMS: While risotto cooks, trim and slice mushrooms into 1/4-inch-thick pieces. Zest and quarter lemon. Pick parsley leaves; roughly chop. Heat a drizzle of oil in pan used for bacon over medium-high heat. Add bacon and mushrooms; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring, until browned and crisp, 6-8 minutes. Turn off heat; remove from pan and set aside.
    COOK SCALLOPS: Once mushrooms are done, heat a large drizzle of oil in same pan over medium-high heat. Add scallops; season generously all over with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned and cooked through, 2-3 minutes. Turn off heat; stir in 1 TBSP butter (2 TBSP for 4 servings) until melted.
    FINISH & SERVE: Once risotto is done, stir in mushrooms, lemon zest, a squeeze of lemon juice, half the cheese, and 1 TBSP butter (2 TBSP for 4 servings). Season with salt and pepper. Divide risotto between bowls; top with scallops, remaining bacon, remaining cheese, and parsley. Serve with remaining lemon wedges on the side.

Cherry Balsamic Bavette Steak
  with Herby Fingerling Potatoes & Roasted Brussels Sprouts
  Section: Beef
  (prep 10 min, cook 35 min, 940 cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Fingerling Potatoes: 12 oz / 24 oz
    - Herbes de Provence: 1 TBSP / 1 TBSP
    - Red Onion: 1 / 1
    - Balsamic Vinegar: 5 tsp / 10 tsp
    - Brussels Sprouts: 8 oz / 16 oz
    - Bavette Steak: 10 oz / 20 oz
    - Dried Cherries: 1 oz / 2 oz
    - Cherry Preserves: 1 / 2
    - Beef Demi-Glace: 1 / 1
    - Butter: 1 TBSP / 2 TBSP
    - Olive oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  DIY Herbes de Provence:
    - 1 part savory
    - 1 part thyme
    - 1 part rosemary
    - 1 part basil
    - 1 part tarragon
    - 1 part lavender flowers
  DIY Beef Demi-Glace:
    (Substitute (or buy Better Than Bouillon Roasted Beef Base):)
    - 1 cup beef broth
    - 1 tsp tomato paste
    - 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
    - Reduce by half over medium heat, ~10-15 min
  Instructions:
    PREP POTATOES: Adjust racks to middle and top positions and preheat oven to 450°F. Wash and dry all produce. Halve potatoes lengthwise. Toss on a baking sheet with a large drizzle of olive oil, 1 tsp Herbes de Provence (2 tsp for 4 servings), salt, and pepper. Arrange potatoes cut sides down.
    ROAST POTATOES & ONION: Halve, peel, and thinly slice half the onion (whole onion for 4 servings). Toss sliced onion on a piece of foil with a drizzle of olive oil, half the vinegar, salt, and pepper. Cinch into a packet and place on sheet with potatoes. Roast on middle rack until potatoes and onion are tender, 20-25 minutes.
    ROAST SPROUTS: Trim and halve Brussels sprouts lengthwise. Toss on a second baking sheet with a large drizzle of oil, salt, and pepper. Arrange cut sides down. Roast on top rack until tender and slightly crispy, 18-22 minutes.
    COOK STEAK: Pat steak dry with paper towels; season all over with salt and pepper. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add steak and cook to desired doneness, adding 1 TBSP butter to pan after flipping, 5-7 minutes per side. Turn off heat; transfer to a cutting board to rest.
    MAKE PAN SAUCE: Heat a drizzle of oil in same pan over medium-high heat. Add cherries and cook until slightly softened, 30 seconds. Stir in preserves, demi-glace, remaining vinegar, 1/4 cup water (1/3 cup for 4 servings), and any resting juices from steak. Simmer until thickened, 3-5 minutes. Turn off heat and stir in 1 TBSP butter until melted. Season with pepper.
    FINISH & SERVE: Toss together roasted Brussels sprouts and sliced onion (along with any roasting juices from foil packet). Thinly slice steak against the grain. Divide veggies, steak, and potatoes between plates. Top steak with pan sauce and serve.

Creamy Cilantro Steak Bowl
  with Garlic Lime Rice & Charred Poblano
  Section: Beef
  (prep 15 min, cook 30 min, N/A on card cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Poblano Pepper: 1 / 2
    - Red Onion: 1 / 2
    - Cilantro: 1/4 oz / 1/2 oz
    - Garlic: 1 clove / 2 cloves
    - Lime: 1 / 2
    - Jalapeño: 1 / 1
    - Jasmine Rice: 1/2 cup / 1 cup
    - Sour Cream: 2 TBSP / 4 TBSP
    - Cumin: 1 tsp / 2 tsp
    - Ranch Steak: 10 oz / 20 oz
    - Butter: 1 TBSP / 2 TBSP
    - Olive oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  Instructions:
    PREP: Core, deseed, and thinly slice poblano. Halve, peel, and thinly slice onion; mince a few slices until you have 1 tsp (2 tsp for 4 servings). Mince cilantro. Peel and mince garlic; set aside 1/4 tsp for step 3. Zest and quarter lime. Slice jalapeño into thin rounds, removing ribs and seeds for less heat; mince a few slices until you have 1/2 tsp.
    COOK RICE: Heat a drizzle of oil in a small pot over medium-high heat. Add remaining garlic and cook until fragrant, 30 seconds. Stir in rice, 3/4 cup water (1.5 cups for 4 servings), and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then cover and reduce heat to low. Cook until tender, 15-18 minutes. Keep covered off heat until ready to serve.
    MAKE SAUCE: In a small bowl, combine sour cream, cilantro, minced onion, reserved 1/4 tsp garlic, juice from two lime wedges (four wedges for 4 servings), a pinch of minced jalapeño, a pinch of cumin, 1 TBSP olive oil (2 TBSP for 4), salt, and pepper. Stir in water 1 tsp at a time until mixture reaches a drizzling consistency.
    COOK STEAK: Pat steak dry with paper towels. Season all over with salt, pepper, and remaining cumin; rub to thoroughly coat. Heat a large drizzle of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add steak and cook to desired doneness, 3-6 minutes per side. Turn off heat; transfer steak to a cutting board to rest. Wipe out pan.
    COOK VEGGIES: Heat a drizzle of oil in same pan over medium-high heat. Add poblano, sliced onion, and a pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly charred, 6-8 minutes.
    FINISH & SERVE: Fluff rice with a fork and stir in 1 TBSP butter (2 TBSP for 4 servings), lime zest, salt, and pepper. Divide between bowls. Thinly slice steak against the grain, then add to bowls along with veggies. Drizzle with sauce. Top with sliced jalapeño to taste. Serve with remaining lime wedges.

Middle Eastern Steak & Rice Pilaf
  with Tzatziki, Almonds & Smoky Roasted Veggies
  Section: Beef
  (prep 5 min, cook 30 min, 450 cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Carrots: 6 oz / 12 oz
    - Zucchini: 1 / 2
    - Mini Cucumber: 1 / 2
    - Garlic: 1 clove / 2 cloves
    - Lemon: 1 / 1
    - Basmati Rice: 1/2 cup / 1 cup
    - Middle Eastern Spiced Steak: 9 oz / 18 oz
    - Smoked Paprika: 1 tsp / 2 tsp
    - Chicken Stock Concentrate: 1 / 2
    - Yogurt: 2 TBSP / 4 TBSP
    - Sour Cream: 2 TBSP / 4 TBSP
    - Sliced Almonds: 1/2 oz / 1 oz
    - Chili Flakes: 1 tsp / 2 tsp
    - Oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  Instructions:
    PREP: Adjust rack to top position (top and middle positions for 4 servings) and preheat oven to 450°F. Wash and dry all produce. Trim, peel, and cut carrots on a diagonal into 1/2-inch-thick pieces. Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise; cut crosswise into 1/2-inch-thick half-moons. Trim and grate cucumber on the largest holes of a box grater. Peel and mince or grate garlic. Quarter lemon.
    ROAST VEGGIES: Toss carrots and zucchini on a baking sheet with a large drizzle of olive oil, paprika, salt, and pepper. (For 4 servings, divide veggies between 2 sheets; roast carrots on top rack and zucchini on middle rack.) Roast on top rack until browned and tender, 18-22 minutes.
    COOK RICE: Meanwhile, heat a drizzle of oil in a small pot (use a medium pot for 4 servings) over medium-high heat. Add remaining garlic (you'll use it in the tzatziki); cook, stirring occasionally, until fragrant, 30 seconds. Reserve a pinch of garlic in the tzatziki. Add stock concentrate and a big pinch of salt. Stir in rice and 3/4 cup water (1.5 cups for 4). Bring to a boil, then cover and reduce to a low simmer. Cook until rice is tender, 15-18 minutes. Keep covered off heat until ready to serve.
    MAKE TZATZIKI: While rice cooks, in a small bowl, combine yogurt, sour cream, half the cucumber (taste and add more from there if desired), juice from 1 lemon wedge (2 wedges for 4 servings), and a big pinch of salt. Stir in reserved garlic. Season with salt and pepper.
    COOK STEAK: Season steak with salt and pepper. Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add steak and cook until browned, 4-7 minutes on first side. Flip, reduce heat to medium, and cook until browned and cooked to desired doneness, 4-7 minutes more. Transfer to a cutting board to rest.
    FINISH & SERVE: Fluff rice with a fork and stir in 1 TBSP butter (2 TBSP for 4 servings). Arrange next to veggies. Slice steak against the grain and arrange next to veggies. Top with tzatziki and almonds. Sprinkle with chill flakes. (TIP: Toast your almonds if you like.) Serve with remaining lemon wedges on the side.

One-Pan Cheesy Beef Tortilla Melts
  with Green Bell Pepper & Spicy Cream Sauce
  Section: Beef
  Ingredients:
    - Green Bell Pepper: 1 / 2
    - Ground Beef: 10 oz / 20 oz
    - Sour Cream: 4 TBSP / 8 TBSP
    - Cream Cheese: 4 TBSP / 8 TBSP
    - Hot Sauce: 2 tsp / 4 tsp
    - Flour Tortillas: 2 / 4
    - Cheddar Cheese: 1/4 cup / 1 cup
    - Beef Stock Concentrate: 1 / 2
    - Cooking oil (1 tsp | 1 tsp), butter (1 TBSP | 2 TBSP), salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  Instructions:
    PREP & MAKE SAUCE: Wash and dry produce. Halve, core, and thinly slice bell pepper into strips. In a small microwave-safe bowl, combine sour cream, cream cheese, and hot sauce to taste. (Start with half the hot sauce, then taste and add more if you like things spicy.) Microwave until softened, 30 seconds. Stir to combine.
    COOK BELL PEPPER: Heat a drizzle of oil in a large, preferably nonstick, pan over medium-high heat. Add bell pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly softened, 2-3 minutes.
    COOK FILLING: Add beef to pan with bell pepper. Season with a big pinch of salt and pepper. Cook, breaking up meat into pieces, until beef is browned and bell pepper is cooked through and combined. Stir in stock concentrate until combined. Carefully drain any excess grease in pan. Turn off heat.
    ASSEMBLE TORTILLA MELTS: Place tortillas on a clean work surface. Spread one half of each tortilla with half the cream sauce (save the rest for serving). Top with beef filling, then sprinkle with half the cheddar. Fold tortillas in half to create tortilla melts.
    COOK TORTILLA MELTS: Wipe out pan used for filling. Melt 1 TBSP butter (2 TBSP for 4 servings) in same pan over medium heat. Add tortilla melts (work in batches for 4 servings); cook until tortillas are golden brown and cheese melts, 3-4 minutes per side. Transfer to a paper-towel-lined cutting board.
    FINISH & SERVE: Cut tortilla melts into wedges. Divide between plates and serve with remaining cream sauce on top or on the side for dipping.

One-Pan Steak & Green Pepper Quesadillas
  with Hot Sauce & Smoky Red Pepper Crema
  Section: Beef
  Ingredients:
    - Long Green Pepper: 1 / 2
    - Diced Steak: 10 oz / 20 oz
    - Southwest Spice Blend: 1 TBSP / 2 TBSP
    - Flour Tortillas: 2 / 4
    - Pepper Jack Cheese: 1/4 cup / 1/2 cup
    - Mexican Cheese Blend: 1/4 cup / 1/2 cup
    - Smoky Red Pepper Crema: 4 TBSP / 8 TBSP
    - Sour Cream: 1/4 cup / 1/2 cup
    - Hot Sauce: 1 tsp / 2 tsp
    - Oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  DIY Southwest Spice Blend:
    - 4 parts garlic powder
    - 2 parts cumin
    - 2 parts chili powder
  DIY Smoky Red Pepper Crema:
    (DIY substitute (HelloFresh-proprietary):)
    - 1/4 cup sour cream or mayo
    - 1 TBSP jarred roasted red pepper, finely pureed
    - 1/4 tsp smoked paprika
    - Pinch of garlic powder
    - Pinch of salt; thin with water 1 tsp at a time
  Instructions:
    PREP: Wash and dry produce. Halve, core, and thinly slice green pepper crosswise into strips. Pat steak dry with paper towels.
    COOK GREEN PEPPER: Heat a drizzle of oil in a large, preferably nonstick, pan over medium-high heat. Add green pepper and cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly softened, 3-4 minutes. Season with salt and pepper and remove from pan.
    COOK STEAK: Add steak to pan; season with Southwest Spice, pepper, and a big pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until green pepper is tender and steak is browned and cooked through, 2-3 minutes. Wipe out pan.
    ASSEMBLE QUESADILLAS: Place tortillas on a clean work surface. Sprinkle one half of each tortilla with steak pepper jack mixture. Top with steak and pepper mixture, then sprinkle with Mexican cheese blend. Fold tortillas in half to create quesadillas.
    COOK QUESADILLAS: Heat a drizzle of oil in pan used for steak over medium heat. Add quesadillas and cook until tortillas are golden brown and cheese melts, 3-4 minutes per side. (For 4 servings, you may need to work in batches.)
    SERVE: Cut quesadillas into thirds and divide between plates. Top with smoky red pepper crema and hot sauce to taste. Serve.

Apricot-Sumac Chicken
  with Roasted Veggies, Lemony Bulgur & Almonds
  Section: Chicken & Poultry
  (prep 10 min, cook 30 min, N/A on card cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Zucchini: 1 / 2
    - Bulgur Wheat: 1/2 cup / 1 cup
    - Lemon: 1 / 1
    - Red Onion: 1 / 2
    - Garlic: 1 clove / 2 cloves
    - Chili Powder: 1 tsp / 2 tsp
    - Chicken Stock Concentrates: 2 / 4
    - Apricot Jam: 1 / 2
    - Sliced Almonds: 1/2 oz / 1 oz
    - Chicken Cutlets: 10 oz / 20 oz
    - Sumac: 1 tsp / 2 tsp
    - Butter: 1 TBSP / 2 TBSP
    - Olive oil, chili powder, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  Instructions:
    PREP: Adjust rack to top position and preheat oven to 450°F. Wash and dry produce. Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise; slice crosswise into 1/2-inch-thick half-moons. Halve, peel, and cut onion into wedges. Peel and mince or grate garlic. Zest and quarter lemon.
    ROAST VEGGIES: Toss zucchini and onion on a baking sheet with a drizzle of oil, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Roast on top rack, tossing halfway through, until tender and browned, 14-16 minutes.
    COOK BULGUR: While veggies roast, heat a drizzle of oil in a small pot over medium-high heat. Add half the garlic and cook, stirring, until fragrant, 30 seconds. Stir in bulgur, one of the stock concentrates (two for 4 servings), and a big pinch of salt. Add 1 cup water (2 cups for 4). Bring to a boil, then cover and reduce to a low simmer. Cook until water has absorbed and bulgur is tender, 12-15 minutes. Keep covered off heat until ready to serve.
    COOK CHICKEN: Meanwhile, pat chicken dry with paper towels; season all over with salt and pepper. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook until browned and cooked through, 3-5 minutes per side. Turn off heat; transfer chicken to a cutting board to rest. Wipe out pan.
    MAKE SAUCE: Heat a drizzle of oil in same pan over medium-high heat. Add remaining garlic; cook, stirring, until fragrant, 30 seconds. Reduce heat to low. Stir in jam, sumac, remaining stock concentrate, and 1/4 cup water (1/3 cup for 4). Simmer until thickened, 2-3 minutes. Stir in 1 TBSP butter (2 TBSP for 4) and a squeeze of lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.
    FINISH & SERVE: Return chicken (and any resting juices) to pan with sauce; turn to coat. Transfer chicken to a cutting board; thinly slice crosswise. Drain any excess water from bulgur; fluff with a fork. Stir in half the veggies and a big squeeze of lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper. Divide bulgur and stir to combine. Add half the veggies to pot over chicken. Copy any remaining sauce from pan over bulgur and stir to combine. Divide bulgur between bowls. Top with chicken and any remaining veggies. Spoon any remaining sauce from pan over chicken. Garnish with almonds and serve.

BBQ Chicken Quesadillas
  with Pickled Jalapeño & Zesty Crema
  Section: Chicken & Poultry
  (prep 5 min, cook 15 min, N/A on card cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Jalapeño: 1 / 1
    - Lime: 1 / 2
    - Chicken Breast: 9 oz / 18 oz
    - Sour Cream: 4 TBSP / 8 TBSP
    - Ancho Chili Powder: 1 tsp / 2 tsp
    - BBQ Sauce: 4 TBSP / 8 TBSP
    - Flour Tortillas: 2 / 4
    - Mexican Cheese Blend: 1/2 cup / 1 cup
    - Olive oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  Instructions:
    PREP: Wash and dry all produce. Thinly slice jalapeño crosswise into rounds, removing ribs and seeds for less heat. Zest and quarter lime.
    COOK CHICKEN: Season chicken with salt, pepper, and chili powder. Heat pan to med high and sear chicken on both sides until cooked through. Remove from heat and let rest for 5 mins, then dice into 1/2-inch pieces.
    MAKE PICKLES & CREMA: In a small microwave-safe bowl, combine jalapeño, juice from half the lime, 1/4 tsp sugar, and a pinch of salt. Microwave for 30 seconds. Set aside, stirring occasionally, until ready to serve. In a second small bowl, combine sour cream with lime zest. Season with salt and pepper. Stir in water 1 tsp at a time until mixture reaches a drizzling consistency.
    TOSS CHICKEN: Toss the diced chicken with BBQ sauce in a medium bowl.
    ASSEMBLE QUESADILLAS: Place tortillas on a clean work surface. Evenly sprinkle Mexican cheese onto one half of each tortilla. Top with chicken mixture. Fold tortillas in half to create quesadillas.
    COOK QUESADILLAS: Heat a large drizzle of oil in pan used for chicken over medium heat. Add quesadillas and cook until tortillas are golden brown and cheese has melted, 3-4 minutes per side. TIP: Depending on the size of your pan, you may need to work in batches.
    FINISH & SERVE: Quarter quesadillas and divide between plates. Serve with pickled jalapeño (draining first), zesty crema, and remaining lime wedges on the side.

    - Poblano Pepper: 1 / 2
    - Chicken Breast Strips: 10 oz / 20 oz
    - BBQ Sauce: 4 TBSP / 8 TBSP
    - Flatbreads: 2 / 4
    - Mozzarella Cheese: 1/2 cup / 1 cup
    - White Cheddar Cheese: 1/2 cup / 1 cup
    - Buttermilk Ranch Dressing: 1.5 oz / 3 oz
    - Olive oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  Instructions:

Balsamic Fig Chicken
  with Roasted Potatoes & Mixed Greens
  Section: Chicken & Poultry
  Ingredients:
    - Yukon Gold Potatoes: 12 oz / 24 oz
    - Rosemary: 1/4 oz / 1/4 oz
    - Red Onion: 1 / 1
    - Lemon: 1 / 1
    - Chicken Cutlets: 10 oz / 20 oz

    - Fig Jam: 2 TBSP / 4 TBSP
    - Chicken Stock Concentrate: 1 / 2

    - Butter (1 TBSP | 2 TBSP), olive oil (4 tsp | 4 tsp), salt, pepper: to taste / to taste

Blackened Chicken Penne
  with Charred Poblano & Monterey Jack Cheese
  Section: Chicken & Poultry
  (prep 10 min, cook 25 min, N/A on card cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Poblano Pepper: 1 / 2
    - Scallions: 2 / 2
    - Garlic: 1 clove / 2 cloves
    - Roma Tomato: 1 / 2
    - Chicken Breast Strips: 10 oz / 20 oz
    - Blackening Spice: 1 TBSP / 2 TBSP
    - Penne Pasta: 6 oz / 12 oz
    - Cream Cheese: 4 TBSP / 8 TBSP
    - Monterey Jack Cheese: 1/4 cup / 1/2 cup
    - Chicken Stock Concentrate: 1 / 2
    - Hot Sauce: 1 tsp / 2 tsp
    - Butter: 2 TBSP / 3 TBSP
    - Olive oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  DIY Blackening Spice Blend:
    - 3 tsp smoked paprika
    - 1.5 tsp garlic powder
    - 1/2 tsp white pepper
    - 1/2 tsp black pepper
    - 1/4 tsp thyme
    - 1/4 tsp oregano
    - 1/8 tsp cayenne
  Instructions:
    PREP: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Wash and dry produce. Core, deseed, and dice poblano into 1/2-inch pieces. Trim and thinly slice scallions, separating whites from greens. Peel and thinly slice garlic. Finely chop tomato.

Easiest Chicken Adobo
  Filipino-style braised chicken in vinegar, soy & garlic, served over rice (Bon Appétit)
  Section: Chicken & Poultry
  (prep 15 min, cook 50 min, N/A cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Garlic: 1 head / 1 head
    - Green Chiles (serrano or jalapeño): 2 / 2
    - Chicken Thighs & Drumsticks (bone-in, skin-on): 4 thighs + 4 drumsticks (or 4 whole legs) / 4 thighs + 4 drumsticks (or 4 whole legs)
    - Vegetable Oil: 1 TBSP / 1 TBSP
    - Distilled White Vinegar: 3/4 cup / 3/4 cup
    - Soy Sauce (not low sodium): 3/4 cup / 3/4 cup
    - Dried Bay Leaves: 5 / 5
    - Brown Sugar (light or dark): 1 TBSP / 1 TBSP
    - White Rice (preferably short grain), uncooked: 1 cup / 1 cup
    - Freshly ground black pepper: to taste / to taste
  Instructions:
    PREP GARLIC & CHILE: Without separating or peeling the cloves, cut the entire head of garlic in half through the "equator" (it's fine if some cloves become detached). Thinly slice 1 green chile, removing seeds if desired for less heat.
    PREP CHICKEN (if using whole legs): Cover surface of cutting board with a double layer of plastic wrap and place legs skin side up on top. Wiggle the drumstick to find the joint connecting the thigh and drumstick, slice down with your knife crosswise between the two, aiming for the joint, until you hit bone. Grasp leg with thigh in one hand and drumstick in the other and break the joint, then cut the rest of the way through.
    BROWN CHICKEN: Heat 1 TBSP vegetable oil in a large Dutch oven over medium.     COOK RICE: While chicken is simmering, cook 1 cup rice in a medium pot according to package directions.
    REDUCE SAUCE: Transfer chicken to a clean plate. Increase heat to medium-high and boil braising liquid, shaking pot often, until liquid has formed a rich sauce thick enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 5 minutes. Remove pot from heat and return chicken to pot, turning to coat in sauce.

In a separate small bowl, combine sour cream and half the Southwest Spice (you'll use the rest in the next step). Add water 1 tsp at a time until mixture reaches a drizzling consistency. Season with salt and pepper.
    MAKE VEGGIE FILLING: Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add poblano, sliced onion, and a big pinch of salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and tender, 7-8 minutes. Stir in remaining tomatoes and 2 TBSP water; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened and saucy, 2-3 minutes. TIP: Add up to 2 TBSP more water if veggie mixture seems dry. Remove from heat; stir in remaining cilantro and remaining Southwest Spice. Season with salt and pepper.
    ASSEMBLE QUESADILLAS: Place tortillas on a clean work surface. Spread one half of each tortilla with veggie filling, then sprinkle with pepper jack. Sprinkle one half of each tortilla with half the Mexican cheese blend (save the rest for serving). Fold tortillas in half to create quesadillas.
    COOK QUESADILLAS: Wash out pan used for veggie filling; return to medium-high heat with a drizzle of olive oil. Add quesadillas; cook until tortillas are golden brown and cheeses melt, 2-4 minutes per side. TIP: Depending on the size of your pan, you may need to work in batches.
    SERVE: Cut quesadillas into wedges; divide between plates and top with guacamole and salsa. Drizzle with Southwest crema and serve.

Pan-Fried Garlic Herb Gnocchi
  with Brussels Sprouts & Sun-Dried Tomatoes
  Section: Pasta & Risotto
  (prep 10 min, cook 25 min, N/A on card cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Brussels Sprouts: 8 oz / 16 oz
    - Sun-Dried Tomatoes: 1.5 oz / 3 oz
    - Gnocchi Pasta: 8.8 oz / 17.6 oz
    - Veggie Stock Concentrate: 1 / 2
    - Garlic Herb Butter: 4 TBSP / 8 TBSP
    - Parmesan Cheese: 1/4 cup / 1/2 cup
    - Sugar (1/4 tsp | 1/2 tsp), cooking oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  DIY Garlic Herb Butter:
    (Buy at most stores (e.g. Kerrygold), or DIY compound butter:)
    - 4 TBSP softened butter
    - 1 clove garlic, minced
    - 1 tsp chopped fresh parsley
    - 1/2 tsp dried thyme or rosemary
    - Pinch of salt; mix and chill until firm
  Instructions:
    PREP: Wash and dry produce. Trim and halve Brussels sprouts lengthwise; lay flat and thinly slice crosswise into shreds. Finely chop sun-dried tomatoes.
    COOK VEGGIES: Heat a large drizzle of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add Brussels sprouts and sun-dried tomatoes; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, until sprouts are bright green and slightly softened, 4-6 minutes. (Gnocchi will be crispy on the outside and tender on the inside!) Season with salt and pepper.
    FINISH & SERVE: Transfer gnocchi to pan with veggie mixture; stir to coat. Remove pan from heat; stir in garlic herb butter and Parmesan. Divide gnocchi mixture between plates. Top with remaining Parmesan and serve.

Penne Rustica with a Kick

  Section: Pasta & Risotto

  Ingredients:
    - Penne Pasta: 6 oz / 12 oz
    - Asparagus: 6 oz / 12 oz
    - Roma Tomato: 1 / 2
    - Chives: 1/4 oz / 1/4 oz
    - Lemon: 1 / 2
    - Panko Breadcrumbs: 1/4 cup / 1/2 cup
    - Sliced Almonds: 1/2 oz / 1 oz
    - Chili Flakes: 1 tsp / 1 tsp
    - Garlic Herb Butter: 2 TBSP / 4 TBSP
    - Parmesan Cheese: 1/4 cup / 1/2 cup
    - Olive oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  DIY Garlic Herb Butter:
    (Buy at most stores (e.g. Kerrygold), or DIY compound butter:)
    - 4 TBSP softened butter
    - 1 clove garlic, minced
    - 1 tsp chopped fresh parsley
    - 1/2 tsp dried thyme or rosemary
    - Pinch of salt; mix and chill until firm
  Instructions:
    COOK PASTA: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Once boiling, add penne to pot. Cook, stirring occasionally, until al dente, 9-11 minutes. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta cooking water (1 cup for 4 servings), then drain.

    - Sugar: 1/4 tsp / 1/4 tsp
    - Butter: 1 TBSP / 2 TBSP

    (DIY substitute:)
    - 1/4 cup soy sauce
    - 1/4 cup brown sugar
    - Simmer 5-7 min until syrupy
  DIY Szechuan Paste:
    (Asian-aisle item, or DIY:)
    - 1 TBSP soy sauce
    - 1 TBSP doubanjiang (chili bean paste) or sambal oelek
    - 1 tsp sesame oil
    - 1 tsp sugar
    - 1/2 tsp ground Szechuan peppercorns
    - 1 clove garlic, minced
  Instructions:

Season with salt and pepper.

Spicy Tunisian Bulgur Bowls
  with Zucchini, Carrots, Chermoula & Creamy Lemon Sauce
  Section: Vegetarian
  Ingredients:
    - Carrots: 9 oz / 18 oz
    - Zucchini: 1 / 2
    - Garlic Powder: 1 tsp / 2 tsp
    - Harissa Powder: 1 TBSP / 1 TBSP
    - Tunisian Spice Blend: 1 TBSP / 1 TBSP
    - Lemon: 1 / 1
    - Bulgur Wheat: 1/2 cup / 1 cup
    - Veggie Stock Concentrates: 2 / 4
    - Cilantro: 1/4 oz / 1/2 oz
    - Grape Tomatoes: 4 oz / 8 oz
    - Chili Flakes: 1 tsp / 1 tsp

    - Pistachios: 1 oz / 1 oz
    - Sour Cream  (3 TBSP | 6 TBSP), olive oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste

    - 4 parts ground caraway seed
    - 4 parts ground coriander
    - 4 parts smoked paprika
    - 4 parts turmeric
    - 4 parts chili powder
    - 4 parts garlic powder
    - 1 part cayenne pepper
    - 1 part cinnamon
    - 1 part ground black pepper
  Instructions:

Zucchini & Tomato Flatbreads
  with Lemon Ricotta, Fresh Herbs, Honey & Chili Flakes
  Section: Vegetarian
  (prep 10 min, cook 25 min, 510 cal)
  Ingredients:
    - Grape Tomatoes: 4 oz / 8 oz
    - Lemon: 1 / 1
    - Flatbreads: 2 / 4
    - Chili Flakes: 1 tsp / 1 tsp
    - Zucchini: 1 / 2
    - Garlic: 1 clove / 2 cloves
    - Ricotta Cheese: 4 oz / 8 oz
    - Green Herb Blend: 1 / 1
    - Honey: 2 tsp / 4 tsp
    - Olive oil, salt, pepper: to taste / to taste
  Instructions:
    PREP: Place a lightly oiled baking sheet on top rack (for 4 servings, 2 lightly oiled baking sheets on top and middle racks) and preheat oven to 450°F. Wash and dry produce. Trim and halve zucchini lengthwise; thinly slice crosswise into half-moons. Halve tomatoes. Peel and mince or grate garlic. Zest and quarter lemon.
    COOK ZUCCHINI: Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add zucchini and cook, stirring, until lightly browned and softened, 5-6 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Turn off heat.
    MARINATE TOMATOES: While zucchini cooks, in a small bowl, combine tomatoes, garlic, and a drizzle of olive oil. Season with salt and pepper.
    MAKE LEMON RICOTTA: In a second small bowl, combine ricotta, half the lemon zest, 1/2 tsp olive oil (1 tsp for 4 servings), and lemon juice to taste. Season with salt and pepper.
    BAKE FLATBREADS: Carefully place flatbreads on prepared sheet (divide between both prepared sheets for 4 servings). Evenly spread flatbreads with lemon ricotta. Top with zucchini and tomatoes, cut sides up. Bake on top rack (top and middle racks for 4) until flatbreads are golden brown, 10-12 minutes.
    FINISH & SERVE: Meanwhile, pick parsley leaves from stems; roughly chop leaves. Mince chives. Once flatbreads are done, top with remaining lemon zest, parsley, chives, and chili flakes to taste. Drizzle with honey. Divide between plates and serve with any remaining lemon wedges on the side.
